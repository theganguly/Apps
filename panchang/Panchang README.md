> **Document purpose** – Everything printed in the *Harilal Printing Press (Rajkot) Panchang, Vikram Samvat 2081*

## 0. How to read this document (confidence legend)

The source is a set of **phone photographs of the printed book** (curved pages, shadows, small Gujarati digits).
Hand-written pen marks and pen lines were ignored as requested. To honour "make no mistake", every statement carries
an implicit confidence level. Where it matters it is tagged:

| Tag | Meaning |
|-----|---------|
| ✅ **VERIFIED** | Reproduced by the reference engine in this README and compared against the book (or against published astronomical data). |
| 📖 **LEGIBLE** | Read directly from the scan; also consistent with standard Jyotish/Panchang tradition. |
| ⚠️ **VERIFY** | Digits or words were hard to read in the scan, or the book's rule is regional. Re-check against the printed page before shipping as data. |
| 🧮 **FORMULA** | Mathematical rule (no data transcription involved). |

**Golden rule for the app:** never hard-code printed numbers; *compute* them with the engine (Part 10), and use
the book only as a **regression test** (Part 4.9).

### 0.1 What was NOT transcribed digit-by-digit (and why)
* The 24 monthly pages (pp. 52‑75) hold ≈ 360 day-rows × 12 columns of small Gujarati numerals (times in `ક.મિ.`).
  They are **described structurally** and are **regenerated by the engine**; individual cells are not copied.
* The daily planet tables (pp. 40‑51; Sun…Pluto, 5:30 AM IST) – same treatment.
* Lagna Sarani (p. 78), Navamsa table (p. 78), Gorakh‑ank table (p. 39), rotated 36‑guna matching plate (last page) – structure and
  derivation rules are given; numbers are generated by formula where a formula exists.

### 0.2 Legal / data-use note
The index (item 1, p. 1) states that Harilal Press claims a **registered trademark / rights over its panchang diary &
calendar data**. This README therefore documents **methods and public astronomical rules**, and the app should
**compute its own data** (Part 10) rather than copy the book's tables.

## 3. Calendar & time conventions used by the book

### 3.1 The Hindu day (વાર નો દિવસ)
* A *vaar* runs from **local sunrise to the next local sunrise** (not midnight to midnight).
* Anything that happens **before sunrise belongs to the previous vaar** and previous Hindu date.
  *Worked example ✅:* the Sun enters Punarvasu nakshatra at **06‑Jul‑2025 05:46 IST**, before Rajkot sunrise (~06:07),
  so the book's rainfall table (p. 21) lists it under **Saturday 05‑07‑2025** (as read from the scan ⚠️), not Sunday.
* The English date printed in a row is the date on which that *sunrise* falls.

### 3.2 Clock‑time notation `ક.મિ.`
* Hours.minutes on a **24+ hour** clock that restarts at sunrise: `20.22` = 8:22 PM; `25.35` = 1:35 AM *next* English date; `30.00` ≈ 6 AM next day.
* Convert for display: `h ≥ 24 → (h − 24):mm next date`.
* A value like `૩૦.૦૦` in a nakshatra/yoga/karan column means "lasts until the next sunrise or later".

### 3.5 Amānta vs Pūrnimānta, and the Gujarati year start
* **Amānta**: a lunar month runs *new moon → next new moon*; it is *named* by the Sun's sign at the start (§5.7).

* Gujarat's year starts the day after Diwali (Kartak Sud 1) – the day after the Aaso *Amavasya*.

## 4. Astronomical engine – how every number is computed

Everything in a panchang reduces to **three longitudes as a function of time**:

```
λ☉(t)  = Sun's sidereal longitude        λ☾(t) = Moon's sidereal longitude        t = instant (UT)
```
plus the **local sunrise/sunset** for the chosen place. Every printed item (tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karan, rashi, sankranti,
eclipses, muhurats…) is *"find the instant when some function of λ☉, λ☾ crosses a boundary"* (§4.8).

Pipeline:

```
civil date + place ──► JD(UT) ──► JDE = JD + ΔT/86400 ──► Sun, Moon (tropical, mean equinox of date)
                                                          └─ minus Lahiri ayanamsa ──► sidereal λ☉, λ☾
sunrise/sunset  ◄── solar altitude = −0.8333° (centre‑of‑disc 16′ + refraction 34′)
```

### 4.1 Julian Day (Meeus ch. 7) 🧮
```
if M ≤ 2: Y -= 1; M += 12
A = floor(Y/100);  B = 2 − A + floor(A/4)        # Gregorian (date ≥ 1582‑10‑15), else B = 0
JD = floor(365.25(Y+4716)) + floor(30.6001(M+1)) + D + hour/24 + B − 1524.5
```
Test ✅: 2000‑01‑01 12:00 UT → **2451545.0**; 1987‑01‑27 0h → 2446822.5; 1957‑10‑04 19:26 → 2436116.31.
India Standard Time: `UT = IST − 5h30m`.

### 4.2 ΔT (TT − UT) 🧮
Dynamical time is needed by the Sun/Moon theories. Use the **Espenak & Meeus polynomials** (piece‑wise, in code §10).
For 2005‑2050: `ΔT = 62.92 + 0.32217·t + 0.005589·t²`, `t = year − 2000` (≈ 74 s in 2024; observed 69 s – the 5 s
error shifts the Moon by < 0.00003°, irrelevant for panchang). For other years the engine uses the full table.

### 4.3 Sun (Meeus ch. 25, accuracy ≈ 0.01°) 🧮 ✅
```
T   = (JDE − 2451545)/36525
L0  = 280.46646 + 36000.76983 T + 0.0003032 T²        (mean longitude)
M   = 357.52911 + 35999.05029 T − 0.0001537 T²        (mean anomaly)
C   = (1.914602 − 0.004817T − 0.000014T²) sin M + (0.019993 − 0.000101T) sin 2M + 0.000289 sin 3M
λ_true = L0 + C
λ_app  = λ_true − 0.00569 − 0.00478 sin(125.04 − 1934.136 T)      (aberration, nutation)
```
Test ✅ (Meeus ex. 25.a): JDE 2448908.5 → true 199.90988°, apparent 199.90895°. Engine returns 199.909873 / 199.908942.

> **Accuracy note.** 0.01° of Sun error = ≈ 15 minutes on a *sankranti* time and ≈ 1 minute on a *tithi* time (relative speed 12.2°/day).
> For production‑grade sankranti minutes use VSOP87 or the Swiss/JPL ephemeris (§4.9).

### 4.4 Moon (Meeus ch. 47, accuracy ≈ 10″) 🧮 ✅
Mean elements (degrees, T as above):
```
L′ = 218.3164477 + 481267.88123421 T − 0.0015786 T² + T³/538841 − T⁴/65194000
D  = 297.8501921 + 445267.1114034  T − 0.0018819 T² + T³/545868 − T⁴/113065000

A1 = 119.75 + 131.849 T ;  A2 = 53.09 + 479264.290 T
λ☾ = L′ + [ Σ cᵢ·Eᵏ·sin(aᵢD + bᵢM + cᵢ′M′ + dᵢF) + 3958 sin A1 + 1962 sin(L′−F) + 318 sin A2 ] / 10⁶
```

### 4.5 Ayanamsa – Lahiri (Chitrapaksha) 🧮 ✅
Lahiri is defined by the Indian Calendar Reform Committee: **23°15′00.658″ on 21‑Mar‑1956** (true‑equinox value).
Mean value (used with mean longitudes):

### 4.7 Sunrise / sunset 🧮 ✅
```
altitude h(t) = asin( sinφ·sinδ(t) + cosφ·cosδ(t)·cos H(t) ),   H = GMST(t) + λ_east − RA(t)
sunrise  = root of  h(t) = −0.8333°  on the rising side;   sunset = same on the setting side   (bisection, 60 iterations)
GMST(deg) = 280.46061837 + 360.98564736629 (JD − 2451545) + 0.000387933 T² − T³/38710000
RA, δ from λ_app and obliquity ε = 23.439291° − 0.0130042 T
```
Engine for **Rajkot (22.3039° N, 70.8022° E)**: 02‑11‑2024 **06:51:35 / 18:08:52**; 30‑03‑2025 06:41 / 19:01; 21‑06‑2025 06:03 / 19:33; 22‑10‑2025 06:46 / 18:16.
Choices to expose: *upper limb vs centre of disc*, refraction, and **altitude of the observer** (hill stations). The book's convention is the classical sunrise (upper limb).

### 4.8 Event finding (the one algorithm behind everything) 🧮 ✅
Given a smooth, monotonic function `f(t)` (mod 360): *"when does f reach X?"*
1. Coarse step (30 min) until `f` crosses `X` (using `(f−X+180) mod 360 − 180`).
2. **Bisection** on the bracket until the interval is < 1 µday (≈ 0.1 s).
3. Convert JD → IST.

| Question | f(t) | X |
|----------|------|---|
| End of current tithi `n` | elongation `D = λ☾ − λ☉` (mod 360) | `12n` |
| End of karana `k` | `D` | `6k` |
| End of nakshatra `n` | sidereal `λ☾` | `n·13°20′` |
| End of yoga `y` | `(λ☉+λ☾) mod 360` (sidereal) | `y·13°20′` |
| Moon changes rashi | `λ☾` | `30·r` |
| Sun sankranti into rashi `r` | sidereal `λ☉` | `30·r` |
| New / full moon | `D` | 0 / 180 |
| Sun enters nakshatra (rain nakshatra) | `λ☉` | `n·13°20′` |
| Ayana / ritu / equinox | **tropical** `λ☉` | 0°, 30°, 90°, 150°, 210°, 270°, 330° |

**Day‑level procedure** (what a printed row is):
```
for each civil date d:   sr = sunrise(d);   state(d) = (tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karan, moon‑rashi) evaluated AT sr
end‑times = next crossing after sr          (these are the ક.મિ. numbers; subtract the date's midnight, add 24 if next day)
```

### 4.10 Eclipses (p. 28)
Solar/lunar eclipse **instants** come from the Moon–Sun–node geometry:
* An eclipse is possible when a new/full moon occurs within ≈ 18.5° (solar) / 12.2° (lunar) of a node (`|λ☾ − Ω|` or `|λ☾ − Ω − 180|`).
* Greatest eclipse for the Moon: minimise the angular separation between the Moon and the **Earth's shadow axis**
  (anti‑solar point), contacts at separations `0.5·(σ_penumbra/umbra) + moon_radius` (Danjon enlargement 1.02).
* Printed per eclipse (p. 28): **ગ્રહણ સ્પર્શ / મધ્ય / મોક્ષ** (first contact / greatest / last contact) in `ક.મિ.`, the **તેજસ્વિતા (magnitude)**,
  the visible regions, whether it is visible in India (→ *sutak* applies, §9.9). Use Swiss `swe.lun_eclipse_when` / `swe.sol_eclipse_when_loc`.

## 5. The five limbs (પંચાંગ) and month/year logic

Let `λ☉`, `λ☾` be **sidereal** longitudes (degrees, 0‑360) and `D = (λ☾ − λ☉) mod 360` the Moon–Sun elongation.

| Limb (અંગ) | Definition | Count | Span |
|-----------|-----------|-------|------|
| **તિથિ** Tithi | `⌊D / 12°⌋ + 1` | 30 | 12° of elongation (19 h 59 m … 26 h 47 m) |
| **વાર** Vaar | weekday of the **sunrise** | 7 | — |
| **નક્ષત્ર** Nakshatra | `⌊λ☾ / 13°20′⌋ + 1` | 27 | 13°20′ (≈ 19–26 h) |
| **યોગ** Yoga | `⌊((λ☉+λ☾) mod 360) / 13°20′⌋ + 1` | 27 | 13°20′ of the *sum* |
| **કરણ** Karan | `⌊D / 6°⌋ + 1` | 60 (11 names) | 6° = half a tithi |

### 5.1 Tithi (તિથિ) 🧮 ✅
* Tithi 1‑15 = **શુક્લ પક્ષ / સુદ** (waxing); 16‑30 = **કૃષ્ણ પક્ષ / વદ** (waning). Tithi 15 = **પૂર્ણિમા**, tithi 30 = **અમાવસ્યા** (printed "વદ ૩૦" or "૩૦").
* Names (p. 38): 1 એકમ/પ્રતિપદા, 2 બીજ/દ્વિતીયા, 3 ત્રીજ/તૃતીયા, 4 ચોથ/ચતુર્થી, 5 પાંચમ/પંચમી, 6 છઠ્ઠ/ષષ્ઠી, 7 સાતમ/સપ્તમી, 8 આઠમ/અષ્ટમી, 9 નોમ/નવમી,
  10 દસમ/દશમી, 11 અગિયારસ/એકાદશી, 12 બારસ/દ્વાદશી, 13 તેરસ/ત્રયોદશી, 14 ચૌદસ/ચતુર્દશી, 15 પૂનમ/પૂર્ણિમા, 30 અમાસ/અમાવસ્યા.
* **Which tithi names the day?** The tithi **prevailing at local sunrise**. The *ending time* is the next crossing of `D = 12n`.
* **Tithi kshaya (ક્ષય)** – a tithi starts after one sunrise and ends before the next: it never sees a sunrise and is *skipped* (the book writes `3/4` in one row and says e.g. "…નો ક્ષય").
* **Tithi vriddhi (વૃદ્ધિ)** – a tithi that spans two sunrises appears on two consecutive rows (footnote "…ની વૃદ્ધિ").
* A fortnight has therefore 13‑15 civil days; the month lengths in §1.1 (29 or 30 days) result from this.
* Tithi‑swami (ruling deity) of each tithi: §13.9.

**Derived tithi‑days that need extra rules** (see §8.1‑8.2 for the vrat rules): Ekadashi, Chaturthi (moonrise), Pradosh, Purnima (vrat), Amavasya.

### 5.6 Moon‑rashi (ચંદ્ર રાશિ) and Sun‑rashi 🧮
`rashi = ⌊λ / 30°⌋ + 1`: 1 મેષ, 2 વૃષભ, 3 મિથુન, 4 કર્ક, 5 સિંહ, 6 કન્યા, 7 તુલા, 8 વૃશ્ચિક, 9 ધન, 10 મકર, 11 કુંભ, 12 મીન.
Moon changes rashi every ≈ 2¼ days; the monthly pages print the **time of change** when it occurs during that sunrise‑day.

### 5.7 Lunar months, Adhik/Kshaya, Samvat boundaries 🧮 ✅
**Algorithm (amānta, as used by the book):**
1. Find all new‑moon instants `N_i` (crossings of `D = 0`).
2. Lunation `i` runs `N_i → N_{i+1}`. Its **first civil day** is the first date whose **sunrise is after `N_i`**; its **last civil day** is the last date whose sunrise is before `N_{i+1}`. *(Reproduces all 12 months of the book ✅ §15.)*
3. **Month name = rashi occupied by the Sun at `N_i`** (sidereal):
   `Meen→ચૈત્ર, Mesh→વૈશાખ, Vrushabh→જેઠ, Mithun→અષાઢ, Kark→શ્રાવણ, Simh→ભાદરવો, Kanya→આસો, Tula→કારતક, Vrushchik→માગશર, Dhan→પોષ, Makar→મહા, Kumbh→ફાગણ`.
   (✅ e.g. new moon 01‑11‑2024: Sun in Tula → Kartak; new moon 29‑03‑2025: Sun in Meen → Chaitra.)
4. **Adhik māsa (અધિક માસ)** – the lunation in which the Sun does **not** change rashi (`rashi(N_i) == rashi(N_{i+1})`). It takes the name of the *following* month and is prefixed "અધિક". All auspicious ceremonies are barred in it ("મળમાસ").
5. **Kshaya māsa (ક્ષય માસ)** – a lunation containing two sankrantis (`rashi(N_{i+1}) − rashi(N_i) = 2`); very rare (separated by decades to centuries), the adjacent months are named jointly.
6. Samvat 2081 ✅: the 12 lunations each contain exactly one sankranti (16‑11, 15‑12, 14‑01, 12‑02, 14‑03, 14‑04, 15‑05, 15‑06, 16‑07, 17‑08, 17‑09, 17‑10) → **no adhik/kshaya month**; length **354 days**. (**Samvat 2082 does contain one**: the lunation starting at the new moon of 17‑05‑2026 has the Sun in Vrushabh at both ends → **Adhik Jeth, 17‑05‑2026 → 15‑06‑2026** – detected by the engine ✅; nija Jeth follows.)
7. **Year start:** Kartak Sud 1 (first sunrise after the Aaso new moon) ✅ 02‑11‑2024. The *year‑entry chart* (p. 19) is cast for the **new‑moon instant**: **01‑11‑2024 18:17 IST** (engine **18:17:40** ✅).
8. **Vriddhi/kshaya of weeks:** none – months simply have 29 or 30 civil days.

### 6.1 Chaughadiya (ચોઘડિયાં) – front page ✅
**Calculation 🧮**
```
day_len   = sunset − sunrise                    night_len = next_sunrise − sunset
one day‑chaughadiya   = day_len / 8              one night‑chaughadiya = night_len / 8
k‑th day slot   = [sunrise + (k−1)·day_len/8,  sunrise + k·day_len/8]        k = 1..8
k‑th night slot = [sunset  + (k−1)·night_len/8, sunset  + k·night_len/8]
```
Book's worked example ✅: *day of 11 h 04 m = 664 min → 664/8 = 83 min = 1 h 23 m per day‑chaughadiya;* for a 12 h 56 m night → 776/8 = 97 min = 1 h 37 m.
When day = night = 12 h each slot is exactly 1 h 30 m. **Must be computed from the actual sunrise/sunset of the place** (the book says so explicitly).

**Name cycle 🧮 ✅** (7 names in this fixed order, repeating):

### 6.2 Hora (હોરા) – p. 35 ✅

* **First hora of the day = lord of the weekday.** Subsequent horas follow the **Chaldean order of descending orbital period**:

### 7.3 Panchak (પંચક) – p. 19 🧮 ✅
* **Panchak = Moon in Kumbh or Meen** (sidereal 300°‑360°) = last 2 padas of Dhanishtha + Shatabhisha + P.Bhadrapada + U.Bhadrapada + Revati = **4½ nakshatras**.
  Book: *"કુંભનો ચંદ્ર જ્યારે પંચક બેસે અને મેષનો ચંદ્ર થાય ત્યારે પંચક ઉતરે"*.
* Lasts ≈ 5 days; occurs once per lunar cycle (≈ 12 per year; printed as the "પંચક બેસે / ઉતરે" table with *date, weekday, ક.મિ.*).
* **Engine example ✅:** first Panchak of Samvat 2081 begins **Sat 09‑11‑2024 23:27** (Moon → Kumbh) and ends **Thu 14‑11‑2024 03:11** (Moon → Mesh).
* **Cautions (book p. 19):** do not cover a roof/slab (ઘર પર છત ભરવી નહીં), do not collect wood/fuel/hay, **no journey to the south**, no buying/repairing a cot/bedstead or sofa, and **at a cremation during Panchak rites (the "pancak vidhi") must be performed** (avoid doing the funeral without it).
* Sub‑types by weekday of beginning (standard, not in the book): Rog (Sun), Raj (Mon), Agni (Tue), Mrityu (Sat), Chor (Fri).

### 7.4 Vinchhudo (વીંછુડો) – p. 19 🧮 ✅
* **Vinchhudo = Moon in Vrushchik** (sidereal 210°‑240°) = **last 15 ghati of Vishakha (pada 4) + Anuradha + Jyeshtha** (3°20′ + 13°20′ + 13°20′ = 30° ✅).
* Printed as "વીંછુડો બેસે / ઉતરે" (ક.મિ. + date + weekday). Engine ✅: first Vinchhudo after 2‑Nov‑2024 = **Sat 02‑11‑2024 23:23 → Tue 05‑11‑2024 09:45**.
* Book's practical note: avoid shubh karya (*marriage, griha‑pravesh, journeys*) during it; **Anuradha nakshatra part is acceptable for shubh karya** ("અનુરાધા નક્ષત્ર શુભ કામોમાં લેવું સારું છે").

### 7.5 Bhadra / Vishti (વિષ્ટિ) – karan #7 🧮
* **Bhadra = Vishti karan**, occurs 8 times a month: Sud 4 (2nd half), Sud 8 (1st), Sud 11 (2nd), Sud 15 (1st), Vad 3 (2nd), Vad 7 (1st), Vad 10 (2nd), Vad 14 (1st). Each lasts ≈ 6 h.
* Window = `[start of karan #, end]` from §4.8; printed in the last column of each monthly row.
* **Where Bhadra "lives" (standard):** Moon in **Kumbh, Meen, Kark, Simh** → on Earth (harmful); **Mesh, Vrushabh, Mithun, Vrushchik** → heaven (harmless); **Kanya, Tula, Dhanu, Makar** → nether world (harmless). Auspicious work is avoided only when Bhadra is on Earth (many panchangs ignore this refinement).
* *Bhadra is good for:* hostile/aggressive acts (litigation, surgery), *bad for:* marriage, Rakhi tying, griha pravesh, journeys.
* **Raksha‑bandhan rule:** tie the rakhi **after Bhadra ends** (Samvat 2081: Shravan Sud 15, Sat 09‑08‑2025).

### 7.6 Ganda‑mool / Gandanta (p. 38) 🧮
* **ગંડમૂળ નક્ષત્ર**: Ashwini, Ashlesha, Magha, Jyeshtha, Mula, Revati – a child born in them needs *shanti*.
* **ગંડાંત**: the junction zones between a water sign and a fire sign: last 48 min (2 ghati) of Ashlesha & first 48 min of Magha, same for Jyeshtha→Mula and Revati→Ashwini (λ within ≈ 0°48′ of 120°, 240°, 0°). (Gandanta also exists for tithi and lagna in the standard texts ⚠️.)
* **Rahu‑Ketu related:** *"ગંડ યોગ: અંધારી ચૌદસ, અમાસ, આશ્લેષા, જ્યેષ્ઠા, મૂળ"* (p. 38) – birth in these requires vidhi.

### 7.7 Dinshuddhi / Tarabal / Chandrabal 🧮
* **Chandra‑bal (ચંદ્રબળ):** Moon's rashi counted from the person's **janma‑rashi**; good in houses **1, 3, 6, 7, 10, 11**; bad in **4, 8, 12** (book p. 32: *"ચોથા‑આઠમા ચંદ્રની પૂજા કરવી"*, *12th Moon is best for marriage*).
* **Tara‑bal (તારાબળ):** count from the person's **janma‑nakshatra** to the day's nakshatra, divide by 9; remainder → Janma(1), Sampat(2), Vipat(3), Kshema(4), Pratyari(5), Sadhaka(6), Vadha(7), Maitra(8), Param‑maitra(9/0). **Bad: 3, 5, 7.**
* **Dinshuddhi** = *tithi + vaar + nakshatra + yoga + karan + lagna shuddhi* (the "Panch‑ang shuddhi" on p. 34).

## 8. Annual tables

### 8.1 How festival dates are decided (rule families) 🧮
A tithi can touch two civil days, so every festival carries a **rule** saying *at which part of the day the tithi must be present*:

| Rule family | Test | Used for |
|-------------|------|----------|
| **Udaya‑tithi** | tithi at **sunrise** | most vrats, Navratri days, Ganesh Chaturthi (also madhyahna), Vasant Panchami |
| **Madhyahna‑vyapini** | tithi covers **midday** (3rd/4th fifth of the day) | Ram Navami, Ganesh Chaturthi, Parashuram |
| **Aparahna‑vyapini** | tithi covers **afternoon** | Shraddha days, Matsya/Varaha jayanti (book's "અપરાહ્ન" tag) |
| **Pradosh‑vyapini** | tithi present at **sunset + 2h24m** | Holika dahan (also needs no Bhadra), Diwali (Amavasya at pradosh), Dhanteras, Pradosh vrat, Trayodashi |
| **Nishitha‑vyapini** | tithi present at **midnight** | Janmashtami (Krishna), Maha Shivratri, Mahalakshmi |
| **Moonrise** | tithi present at **moonrise** | Sankashti Chaturthi, Karva Chauth, Purnima vrat (book: "વ્રતની પૂનમ") |
| **Smarta vs Vaishnava** | Ekadashi/Janmashtami may fall on different days for Smarta & Vaishnava (Pushtimargi) sampradayas | pp. 26, 30, 31 print both |
| **Solar** | Sun's sankranti | Makar Sankranti, Mesh Sankranti, Karka Sankranti |
| **Nakshatra‑based** | Moon's nakshatra | Dussehra variants, Pushya days, Gurupushyamrut |

**If the tithi touches neither sunrise day (kshaya)** choose the day on which most of the tithi falls (or on which it begins before the required moment). *Worked example ✅:* Yogini Ekadashi 2025 — tithi 11 begins **21‑06 ≈ 07:18** and ends **22‑06 before sunrise**; no sunrise sees it → Smarta observes **21‑06**, Vaishnava (who need Dvadashi at sunrise) observe **22‑06** — exactly the two dates printed on p. 26.

## 9. Muhurat selection rules (pp. 21‑24, 27, 32‑35, 39)

### 9.1 Pancha‑ang shuddhi – the universal five‑point check (p. 34) 📖 ✅
1. **Tithi** – good: 2, 3, 5, 7, 10, 11, 13 (Sud) and Vad 1; **avoid tithi kshaya/vriddhi** days; Rikta (4, 9, 14), Amavasya and Ashtami avoided in most works.

3. **Karan** – good: **તૈતિલ, વણિજ, બવ, બાલવ, કૌલવ, ગર**; avoid **વિષ્ટિ (Bhadra)** and the four fixed karans.

* **Days to avoid:** Vyatipat, Vaidhriti, Mahapat; **kumuhurta periods** (§7.1) – *guru/shukra asta, Holashtak, papagraha‑yukta nakshatra, "karidin" (the 8 days around a sankranti ⚠️), Chaturmas*; "keeping these in mind keeps the day clean".
* **Moon** matters more than Jupiter: *"ગુરૂ ચંદ્રમાં પહોંચે છે કે નહીં તેના કરતાં નક્ષત્રનું ધ્યાન ખાસ રાખવું"*. If the Moon is in the **4th or 8th** from the bride/groom's rashi it is remedied by puja (if the nakshatra is right); **12th Moon is best** for both; in Vad paksha even 4th/8th is fine if tara‑bal is good.
* **Sun–Jupiter–Moon pujya/apujya table** (counted from the *groom's* rashi for Sun, *bride's* rashi for Jupiter, *both* for Moon) — ✅ for Sun & Jupiter, ⚠️ digits for Moon:

| Planet | **Shubh** houses | **Pujya** (worship needed) | **Nishedha** (avoid) |
|--------|-----------------|---------------------------|---------------------|
| Sun (for groom) | 3, 6, 10, 11 | 1, 2, 5, 7, 9 | 4, 8, 12 |
| Jupiter (for bride) | 2, 5, 7, 9, 11 | 1, 3, 6, 10 | 4, 8, 12 |
| Moon (both) | 3, 6, 7, 10, 11, (12) | 1, 2, 5, 9 | 4, 8 |

* **Mangal‑dosh (Manglik)** ⚠️: Mars in **1, 4, 7, 8, 12** of the chart (from Lagna; many also from Moon and Venus) is "Mangal‑dosh". Cancellations: Mars in own/exalted sign, in conjunction/aspect with Jupiter, in Kendra with a strong benefic etc., or both partners manglik.
* **Date selection**: the book's marriage‑muhurat table (pp. 21‑22) gives for each date *tithi, vaar, Moon nakshatra, Moon rashi, Sun rashi and time notes* such as "સાં. ૦૪‑૦૫ થી" (valid from the stated clock time). The app should produce exactly this list with the rules above and mark partial days.
* **Seasonal practice:** Samvat 2081 marriage dates start from Prabodhini Ekadashi (Kartak Sud 11 – 12‑11‑2024) and run through Maha/Fagan (before Holashtak), Vaishakh, Jeth (until Devshayani 06‑07‑2025); Samvat 2082 muhurats begin **Kartak Sud 11 (02‑11‑2025)** (p. 22).
* **Vivah in certain months:** *Magshar, Chaitra (not in Dhanarka/Meenarka), Fagan and Meenarka* – the book says the shastra makes no prohibition for ‘Dhanarka/Meenarka’ in **South India** while in Gujarat they are avoided; likewise "ગુજરાતમાં કમુહૂર્તા વર્જ્ય છે, પરંતુ દક્ષિણ ભારતમાં કમુહૂર્ત લગ્નાદિ કાર્યોમાં વર્જ્ય નથી" (p. 32) ✅. → make `kumuhurta_policy = gujarat | south` a configuration.
* **Eldest child:** in Jeth (with eldest son/daughter) marriage was avoided (traditional ⚠️).

### 9.4 Navvadhu pravesh (new bride's entry) – p. 32 ⚠️
Choose the wedding day itself or an **even‑numbered day** after it, or the **5th, 7th, 9th** (odd days other than these avoided); in the evening/night in a **sthira lagna** (Vrushabh, Simh, Vrushchik, Kumbh) with sthira navamsha; nakshatras Ashwini, Rohini, Mrigashira, Magha, Pushya, U.Phalguni, Chitra, Swati, Anuradha, Mula, U.Ashadha, Shravana, Dhanishtha, U.Bhadrapada, Revati; avoid Rikta tithis; weekdays Mon, Thu, Fri, Sat preferred, **bride should not enter on Wednesday** ("સામાન્ય રીતે કન્યાને બુધવારે પ્રવેશ ન કરાવવો").

### 9.5 Vastu / Griha‑pravesh / Khat‑muhurat (pp. 23, 32‑33) 📖
* **Vastu (કળશ) muhurat tables** (p. 23) list date, vaar, Moon nakshatra, Moon rashi, Sun rashi and notes, tagged "વાસ્તુ" (griha‑pravesh) or "કળશ" (ceremonial kalash/ foundation pot).
* **Shastra quotes** (p. 32): *"વાસ્તુપૂજા મકૃત્વા ન વિશેત્ નવમંદિરે"* (no entry into a new house without Vastu‑puja), *Muhurta Chintamani, Narasimha Samhita*. Classical months for entering a new house: **Magha, Phalguna, Vaishakha, Jyeshtha** (Kartik/Margashirsha with conditions) ⚠️; avoid the *Chaturmas*.
* **Vastu rule from the book:** *Tithi–Vaar–Nakshatra shuddhi + lagna shuddhi*; **Ravi/Mon/Wed/Thu/Fri**; **Rikta tithis (4/9/14) and Amavasya avoided**; 5th, 8th, 6th house of lagna empty.
* **Kuliha, Sutak‑free period**; **panchak** (§7.3) avoid for roof‑casting & south‑facing doors.
* **Khat (foundation‑digging) – Khat chakra (p. 32):** the *location of the day's "earth posture"* is found by
  `S = tithi_number (Vad: +15) + vaar_number (Sun = 1) + nakshatra_number (Ashwini = 1)`, **S ÷ 4 → remainder** (the book names the "Prithvi" posture for each remainder; remainder **1 = Prithvi sitting (બેઠેલી) ⚠️** – confirm others from the page). Dig only when the earth is "sitting", and begin the digging at the **corner** assigned to the **Sun's rashi** group (table on p. 32: Mesh/Simh/Dhan → NE or …, separate rows for *ghar*, *devalay*, *vav‑kuva*, *manek‑stambh* ⚠️). The Sun's rashi/sankranti determines the "sleeping point of the Vastu snake" (Khatmuhurat: ***Kanya, Dhan, Meen*** sankrantis are used for entering).
* **Agni‑chakra 🧮 ✅ (p. 33):** `n = tithi(Sud 1 = 1 … Vad 30 = 30) + vaar(Sun = 1) + 1` ; **n mod 4 ∈ {0, 3} → Agni resides on Earth** (homa/havan allowed, auspicious); **1 → in the Sky, 2 → in Patala** (bad for homa).
* **Ahuti‑chakra 🧮 ✅ (p. 33):** count nakshatras from the **Sun's nakshatra** to the **day's nakshatra** (inclusive); each of 9 "mouths" receives 3 nakshatras in the order *Sun, Mercury, Venus, Saturn, Moon, Mars, Jupiter, Rahu, Ketu*; the offering falls in that graha's mouth; **good if Mercury, Venus, Moon or Jupiter; bad if Sun, Saturn, Mars, Rahu, Ketu** (printed as શ્રે.=shrestha / ને.=neshta).
  `mouth = ((count − 1) // 3) mod 9`. Book exceptions: *Agni‑chakra is not looked at for nemittik (occasional) rites; Kalash‑chakra is looked at for kamya karma*.
* **Practical chakras listed on p. 33** (each is *"count nakshatras from a reference nakshatra to the day's/Moon's nakshatra, assign the count to the printed groups, read **શ્રે (shrestha, good) / ને (neshta, bad)**"*):
  | Chakra | Count from → to | Used for (book's closing line) |
  |--------|-----------------|--------------------------------|
  | ખાતચક્ર / વૃષચક્ર | Sun's nakshatra → Moon's nakshatra (with the Abhijit pattern) | choosing khat (foundation) |
  | દ્વારચક્ર | Sun's nakshatra → Moon's nakshatra | **hanging the door/frame** |
  | મોભચક્ર | Sun's nakshatra → Moon's nakshatra | **raising the main beam (adsar/mob)** |
  | ચૂલ્હીચક્ર | Sun's nakshatra → Moon's nakshatra | placing a new hearth |
  | હળચક્ર | Sun's nakshatra → day's nakshatra (3‑3‑3‑5‑3‑5‑3‑3 pattern) | ploughing new land |
  | કૂપચક્ર | **Rohini** → day's nakshatra | digging a well |
  | પૃથ્વી સૂતી / ચૂડાચક્ર | Sun's nakshatra → day's nakshatra | earth "sleeping" test; wearing the bridal bangle set (ચૂડો) |
  The exact digit patterns (e.g. `૩ ૮ ૭ ૮ ૪ ૫`) are small printed rows on p. 33 and must be keyed from the page ⚠️; the algorithm is always *prefix‑count → group → good/bad*.

Digits ⚠️.

### 10.2 `panchang_core.py`

```python
"""
panchang_core.py  --  reference engine (pure Python, no dependencies)
Sun/Moon: Meeus, Astronomical Algorithms 2e, ch. 25 (Sun, 0.01 deg) and ch. 47 (Moon, ~10 arcsec)
Ayanamsa: Lahiri (Chitrapaksha), mean, anchored at 23 deg 15' 00.658" (true) on 1956-03-21 (ICRC)
"""
import math
from datetime import datetime, timedelta, timezone

D2R = math.pi / 180.0
IST = timezone(timedelta(hours=5, minutes=30))

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- time
def julian_day(y, m, d, hour=0.0):
    """Gregorian (or Julian before 1582-10-15) calendar -> JD (UT)."""
    if m <= 2:
        y -= 1; m += 12
    A = y // 100
    if (y, m, d) >= (1582, 10, 15) or y > 1582:
        B = 2 - A + A // 4
    else:
        B = 0
    return int(365.25 * (y + 4716)) + int(30.6001 * (m + 1)) + d + hour / 24.0 + B - 1524.5

def jd_from_dt(dt):
    dt = dt.astimezone(timezone.utc)
    return julian_day(dt.year, dt.month, dt.day,
                      dt.hour + dt.minute / 60 + dt.second / 3600 + dt.microsecond / 3.6e9)

def dt_from_jd(jd, tz=IST):
    z = math.floor(jd + 0.5); f = jd + 0.5 - z
    if z < 2299161: a = z
    else:
        al = math.floor((z - 1867216.25) / 36524.25); a = z + 1 + al - math.floor(al / 4)
    b = a + 1524; c = math.floor((b - 122.1) / 365.25); d = math.floor(365.25 * c)
    e = math.floor((b - d) / 30.6001)
    day = b - d - math.floor(30.6001 * e)
    month = e - 1 if e < 14 else e - 13
    year = c - 4716 if month > 2 else c - 4715
    secs = round(f * 86400)
    base = datetime(int(year), int(month), int(day), tzinfo=timezone.utc)
    return (base + timedelta(seconds=secs)).astimezone(tz)

def delta_t(year):
    """TT-UT in seconds, Espenak & Meeus polynomials (NASA)."""
    y = year
    if y < -500:  u = (y - 1820) / 100; return -20 + 32 * u * u
    if y < 500:
        u = y / 100
        return 10583.6 - 1014.41*u + 33.78311*u**2 - 5.952053*u**3 - 0.1798452*u**4 + 0.022174192*u**5 + 0.0090316521*u**6
    if y < 1600:
        u = (y - 1000) / 100
        return 1574.2 - 556.01*u + 71.23472*u**2 + 0.319781*u**3 - 0.8503463*u**4 - 0.005050998*u**5 + 0.0083572073*u**6
    if y < 1700: t = y - 1600; return 120 - 0.9808*t - 0.01532*t**2 + t**3/7129
    if y < 1800: t = y - 1700; return 8.83 + 0.1603*t - 0.0059285*t**2 + 0.00013336*t**3 - t**4/1174000
    if y < 1860:
        t = y - 1800
        return 13.72 - 0.332447*t + 0.0068612*t**2 + 0.0041116*t**3 - 0.00037436*t**4 + 0.0000121272*t**5 - 0.0000001699*t**6 + 0.000000000875*t**7
    if y < 1900:
        t = y - 1860
        return 7.62 + 0.5737*t - 0.251754*t**2 + 0.01680668*t**3 - 0.0004473624*t**4 + t**5/233174
    if y < 1920: t = y - 1900; return -2.79 + 1.494119*t - 0.0598939*t**2 + 0.0061966*t**3 - 0.000197*t**4
    if y < 1941: t = y - 1920; return 21.20 + 0.84493*t - 0.076100*t**2 + 0.0020936*t**3
    if y < 1961: t = y - 1950; return 29.07 + 0.407*t - t**2/233 + t**3/2547
    if y < 1986: t = y - 1975; return 45.45 + 1.067*t - t**2/260 - t**3/718
    if y < 2005:

def find_crossing(f, target, jd_lo, jd_hi, period=360.0, tol=1e-7):
    """Find JD where f(jd) (monotonic increasing mod `period`) crosses `target` inside [lo,hi]. Bisection on unwrapped difference."""
    def g(jd):
        v = (f(jd) - target + period/2) % period - period/2
        return v
    lo, hi = jd_lo, jd_hi
    glo, ghi = g(lo), g(hi)
    if not (glo <= 0 <= ghi):  # bracket check
        return None
    while hi - lo > tol:
        mid = 0.5*(lo+hi)
        if g(mid) <= 0: lo = mid
        else: hi = mid
    return 0.5*(lo+hi)

def sun_ra_dec(jde):
    T = (jde - 2451545.0)/36525.0
    lam = sun_longitude_apparent(jde)*D2R
    eps = (23.439291 - 0.0130042*T)*D2R + 0.00256*D2R*math.cos((125.04 - 1934.136*T)*D2R)
    ra = math.atan2(math.cos(eps)*math.sin(lam), math.cos(lam)) % (2*math.pi)
    dec = math.asin(math.sin(eps)*math.sin(lam))
    return ra/D2R, dec/D2R

def sun_altitude(jd, lat, lon):
    jde = jde_from_jd(jd)
    ra, dec = sun_ra_dec(jde)
    H = (gmst_deg(jd) + lon - ra) * D2R
    phi = lat*D2R; d = dec*D2R
    return math.asin(math.sin(phi)*math.sin(d) + math.cos(phi)*math.cos(d)*math.cos(H)) / D2R

def sunrise_sunset(y, m, d, lat, lon, tz_hours=5.5, h0=H0_SUN):
    """Sunrise & sunset (aware datetimes in `tz`) for local civil date y-m-d. Bisection on solar altitude."""
    noon = julian_day(y, m, d, 12.0) - lon/360.0 - 0.0   # approximate local solar noon (UT as JD)
    def bis(a, b):
        fa = sun_altitude(a, lat, lon) - h0
        for _ in range(60):
            mid = 0.5*(a+b); fm = sun_altitude(mid, lat, lon) - h0
            if (fa < 0) == (fm < 0): a, fa = mid, fm
            else: b = mid
        return 0.5*(a+b)
    rise = bis(noon - 0.5, noon)
    sset = bis(noon, noon + 0.5)
    return dt_from_jd(rise), dt_from_jd(sset)

def limb_end(fnum, jd_start, step=1/48):
    """First JD > jd_start at which the integer-valued function fnum(jde) changes."""
    cur = fnum(jde_from_jd(jd_start)); t = jd_start
    while fnum(jde_from_jd(t)) == cur: t += step
    lo, hi = t - step, t
    for _ in range(50):
        mid = 0.5*(lo+hi)
        if fnum(jde_from_jd(mid)) == cur: lo = mid
        else: hi = mid
    return hi
```

## 12. How to read a Janma‑Kundali (જન્મકુંડળી) – complete guide

Sources in the book: **p. 76** (reading rules, house meanings, aspects, yoga relations, rajyoga table, Rahukaal), **p. 77–78** (ayanamsa, lagna sarani, navamsha), **p. 37–38** (avakahada, dashas, friendships, exaltation), **p. 36** (mantras, nadi), **p. 40‑51** (essays on bhava results), **p. 19/46** (yogas). Standard Parashari principles complete what the book summarises (flagged "standard").

### 12.1 What you need
**Date, exact time and place of birth** → convert to UT (`IST − 5:30`) → compute the **sidereal** positions of Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu (and optionally Uranus/Neptune/Pluto) and the **ascendant (lagna)**.
Book's definition: *"જન્મલગન એટલે જે‑તે સ્થળે જે‑તે સમયે પૂર્વ ક્ષિતિજમાં ઉગતી રાશિ."* (the rashi rising on the eastern horizon at that place and time).

### 12.2 Computing the lagna 🧮 ✅
```
RAMC  = GMST(UT) + longitude_east                      (right ascension of the meridian, degrees)
ε     = 23.439291° − 0.0130042·T                       (obliquity)
tan(ASC) = cos(RAMC) / −( sin(RAMC)·cos ε + tan φ · sin ε )          (use atan2 for the correct quadrant)
sidereal lagna = ASC − ayanamsa
```
*Engine check ✅:* at Rajkot sunrise on 02‑11‑2024 the tropical ascendant equals the Sun's tropical longitude to within a few degrees (the old "sunrise lagna" shortcut).
*Year‑entry chart (p. 19):* new moon 01‑11‑2024 18:17:40 IST, **Delhi (28.61° N, 77.21° E)** → engine ascendant **Mesh 29°12′**; the book prints **Mesh lagna 29°35′** — **0°23′ difference** (probably the book's rounded time/coordinates and ayanamsa; Sun & Moon both **Tula 15°23′** ✅, Moon in Swati pada 3).

**The book's table‑based method (p. 78, "સૌરાષ્ટ્ર લગ્નસારણી, અયનાંશ ૨૩"):** rows = Sun's rashi/degree, entries = lagna (rashi‑degree‑kala) at *sunrise*; then add the **lagna durations** to move to birth time: *birth‑time ‑ sunrise* in ghati‑pal → add proportionally to the sunrise lagna using the "lagna‑kal" of each sign. It is an **approximation with a fixed 23° ayanamsa**; use the direct formula above instead.
*Rising time of each sidereal sign at Rajkot (22.30° N), computed with the engine ✅ (≈ ±1 min, varies a little with the date):* Mesh 1h39 · Vrushabh 1h59 · Mithun 2h13 · Kark 2h15 · Simh 2h11 · Kanya 2h09 · Tula 2h13 · Vrushchik ≈ 2h16 · Dhan 2h04 · Makar 1h47 · Kumbh 1h33 · Meen 1h32 (sum = one sidereal day, 23h56m).

**House systems:** Indian charts normally use **Whole‑sign (rashi = bhava)**: house 1 = lagna sign, house 2 = next sign… The book's charts are of this type. *Bhava‑chalit* (equal‑house or Sripati) is a refinement: shift a planet to the neighbouring house if it falls in the first/last part of the bhava (text‑book rule: ±15° around the **bhava madhya**).

### 12.3 Drawing the chart (p. 76)

* Planets are written inside the house whose rashi they occupy. Abbreviations in the book: સૂ. ચં. મં. બુ. ગુ. શુ. શ. રા. કે. + હ. (Harshal), ને. (Neptune), પ્લુ. (Pluto).

### 12.5 Rashi, Nakshatra, Pada, Navamsha (p. 78) 🧮 ✅
* `rashi = ⌊λ/30⌋`, `nakshatra = ⌊λ/13°20′⌋`, `pada = ⌊(λ mod 13°20′)/3°20′⌋ + 1` (engine `rashi_nak_pada`).
* **Navamsha** (D‑9): each rashi is divided into nine parts of **3°20′**; the first navamsha starts at **the same sign for movable (ચર: Mesh, Kark, Tula, Makar), the 9th sign for fixed (સ્થિર: Vrushabh, Simh, Vrushchik, Kumbh), the 5th sign for dual (દ્વિસ્વભાવ: Mithun, Kanya, Dhan, Meen)**, then follows the zodiac. The book's table lists, for each of the 27 nakshatras × 4 padas, the **rashi, degree, kala and navamsha number**; the navamsha of pada *k* of nakshatra *n* is simply `(4(n−1) + k − 1) mod 12 + 1` (Ashwini 1‑2‑3‑4, Bharani 5‑6‑7‑8, Krittika 9‑10‑11‑12 …) ✅ – identical to the table's last row ("નવમાંશ ૧ ૨ ૩ ૪ | ૫ ૬ ૭ ૮ | ૯ ૧૦ ૧૧ ૧૨").
* **Vargottama:** same sign in rashi and navamsha → planet strong.

* **Graha avastha by degree (p. 77 ✅):** in odd signs (Mesh, Mithun, Simh, Tula, Dhan, Kumbh) 0‑6° *Balya (child)*, 6‑12° *Kumara (adolescent)*, 12‑18° *Yuva (youth)*, 18‑24° *Vriddha (old)*, 24‑30° *Mrita (dead)*; in **even** signs (Vrushabh, Kark, Kanya, Vrushchik, Makar, Meen) the order is reversed (24‑30° Balya … 0‑6° Mrita). Youth gives full results, child/adolescent partial, old weak, dead none.
* **Other strength factors:** retrograde (vakri, "chesta‑bala"), combustion (asta), digbala (Jupiter/Mercury in 1st, Sun/Mars in 10th, Saturn in 7th, Moon/Venus in 4th), sthana‑bala (own/exalted), kendradi bala, **ષોડશ વર્ગ** (16 vargas) – book: *"દશાફળ ગ્રહની અવસ્થા, ઉદય, અસ્ત, વક્રી, ષોડશ વર્ગબળ, સ્વગૃહી, મિત્ર, શત્રુ વિ. ઉપર અવલંબે"*.

### 12.7 Aspects (દૃષ્ટિ) – p. 76 ✅
* Every planet **fully aspects the 7th house from itself** (and the planet there).
* **Special aspects:** **Mars 4th and 8th**, **Jupiter 5th and 9th**, **Saturn 3rd and 10th** (counted from the planet's own house). Rahu/Ketu 5, 9 in some schools (not in the book).
* Benefic aspect is good, malefic aspect bad (*"શુભ ગ્રહની દૃષ્ટિ સારી, અશુભ ગ્રહની દૃષ્ટિ સારી નહીં"*).
* **Relations between two planets (book, p. 76) ✅:**

| Relation | Positions (from planet A to B) | Result per book |
|----------|--------------------------------|-----------------|
| **યુતિ** conjunction | same rashi | gives auspicious or inauspicious results |
| **બીયાબારૂ** | 2nd/12th | generally inauspicious |
| **ત્રિ‑એકાદશ** (tri‑ekadash) | 3rd/11th | auspicious |
| **કેન્દ્રયોગ** | 4th/10th | generally inauspicious (as a relation) |
| **નવપંચક** | 5th/9th | auspicious |

### 12.8 Yogas (યોગ)
* **Rajyoga (p. 76):** *"પારાશરી પદ્ધતિ પ્રમાણે નીચેનાં લગ્નોમાં ગ્રહોની યુતિ થતી હોય તો રાજયોગ બને છે."* The page lists, for each lagna, the conjunctions that form rajyoga (e.g. Mesh lagna: Sun+Mars, Sun+Moon, Sun+Venus, Mars+Jupiter, Moon+Jupiter…; Vrushabh: Venus+Saturn, Sun+Mercury… ) and, in the right column, **"નિષ્ફળ યોગ"** (failed combinations) ⚠️ full list needs entry from the page. **Rule behind the table:** *yoga‑karaka = lord of a Kendra + lord of a Trikona in conjunction/aspect/exchange*, with lagna‑specific functional benefics. Effects arrive **during the mahadasha/antardasha** of the yoga planets (book).
* **Standard yogas to implement:** Gajakesari (Jupiter in a Kendra from Moon) · Budha‑Aditya (Sun+Mercury) · Chandra‑Mangala (Moon+Mars) · Dhana yogas (2/5/9/11 lords) · **Pancha‑Mahapurusha** (Ruchaka–Mars, Bhadra–Mercury, Hamsa–Jupiter, Malavya–Venus, Shasha–Saturn: in own/exalted sign **and in a Kendra**) · Neecha‑bhanga · Vipareeta Raja (6/8/12 lords exchange) · Kemadruma (no planet beside Moon) · **Kaal‑sarpa** (all planets between Rahu and Ketu; the book: *"કાલસર્પ યોગ કરતો રાહુ ફળ ઘટાડશે"*) · **Mangal dosha** (§9.3) · **Shakat, Amala, Parvata, Kalanidhi…**
* **Rahu in houses (p. 46) ✅:** Rahu gives good results in the **3rd, 6th and 11th** (same houses as in the gochar rule, p. 26); with the lords of a Kendra/Trikona it can form a *rajyoga*; its conjunction with Jupiter in the 9th/10th etc. is read through the sign‑lord; elsewhere it behaves as a malefic (it takes the results of its sign‑lord and of planets conjoined with it). A **Kaal‑sarpa** formation weakens Rahu's good results (book).
* **Panoti / Sade‑sati:** §8.5. **Dasha lords' houses** decide timing.

### 12.9 Dashas (દશા) – timing (pp. 1, 38) 🧮 ✅
**Vimshottari (120 yr)** – the Moon's nakshatra lord starts:

| Lord | Ketu | Venus | Sun | Moon | Mars | Rahu | Jupiter | Saturn | Mercury |
|------|------|-------|-----|------|------|------|---------|--------|---------|
| Years | 7 | 20 | 6 | 10 | 7 | 18 | 16 | 19 | 17 |
| Nakshatras (3 each) | Ashwini, Magha, Mula | Bharani, P.Phal, P.Ashadha | Krittika, U.Phal, U.Ashadha | Rohini, Hasta, Shravana | Mrigashira, Chitra, Dhanishtha | Ardra, Swati, Shatabhisha | Punarvasu, Vishakha, P.Bhadra | Pushya, Anuradha, U.Bhadra | Ashlesha, Jyeshtha, Revati |
| 1 ghati of the maha‑dasha = | 42 d | 120 d | 36 d | 60 d | 42 d | 108 d | 96 d | 114 d | 102 d |

*(Last row printed on p. 38 as "એક ઘડીનાં દિવસ" = `years × 6` days ✅.)*
1. **Balance at birth** = `lord_years × (1 − fraction_of_nakshatra_elapsed)`, `fraction = (λ☾ mod 13°20′)/13°20′`.
2. Maha‑dashas follow the lord order (Ketu → Venus → Sun → Moon → Mars → Rahu → Jupiter → Saturn → Mercury → Ketu …).
3. **Antardasha** of lord *B* inside maha‑dasha *A* = `years(A) × years(B)/120`, starting with *A* itself.
4. Pratyantar = `A·B·C/120²`; sookshma etc. similarly.
5. Year basis: 365.2425 d (Gregorian) or 360 d (savana) – state it.

**Worked example ✅ (engine output).** Year‑entry instant 01‑11‑2024 18:17:40 IST: Moon = **Tula 15°23′** → **Swati pada 3** (Rahu's nakshatra; 65 % elapsed) → balance **6.22 years of Rahu** → Rahu maha‑dasha ends **22‑01‑2031**; then **Jupiter 16 y → 22‑01‑2047, Saturn 19 y → 22‑01‑2066, Mercury 17 y → 22‑01‑2083**. Navamsha of that Moon: Kumbh; ascendant (Delhi) Mesh 29° in **Krittika pada 1**, navamsha Dhan.

**Ashtottari (108 yr, p. 38):** Sun 6, Moon 15, Mars 8, Mercury 17, Saturn 10, Jupiter 19, Rahu 12, Venus 21 (= 108 ✅); nakshatra grouping printed in the table (⚠️ copy from p. 38); applicable by Parashara when Rahu is not in lagna and is in a Kendra/Trikona from the lagna lord, or for births by day in Krishna paksha / by night in Shukla paksha (standard).
**Din‑dasha (front page):** a *daily* dasha of eight planets based on the Moon's rashi (§2.1).
**Other dashas (standard, not in the book):** Yogini (36 yr), Chara/Jaimini, Kalachakra, Tribhagi.

### 12.10 Step‑by‑step reading procedure (what the book's method amounts to)
1. **Chart check:** rashi chart (D‑1), Moon chart, navamsha (D‑9). Mark planets' houses, aspects, conjunctions.
2. **Lagna:** sign, lord, lord's house/strength, planets in/aspecting lagna (body, temperament, health).
3. **Moon:** rashi, nakshatra, pada (mind; used for dashas, gochar, panoti, naming — `avakahada`, §13.1).
4. **Sun:** soul/authority; combust planets (within ~8°‑17° of the Sun).
5. **Houses one by one:** use §12.4 meanings; read the **house lord**, its placement, aspects; **natural karaka** of the matter; exaltation/debilitation; **avastha** (§12.6).
6. **Yogas** (§12.8) and **doshas** (Mangal, Kaal‑sarpa, Kemadruma, Pitru/Shrapit, Graha‑yuddha) – note cancellations.
7. **Navamsha** for marriage/dharma; **Dashamsha** career, etc. Vargottama and navamsha lord strength.
8. **Dasha**: running maha/antar/pratyantar (§12.9) – results come from the dasha lord's natal houses and lordships.
9. **Gochar (transit)** over natal Moon/lagna (§8.4) with vedha; Sade‑sati / Dhaiya (§8.5).
10. **Annual (varsha‑phal) / Month results** for the year (e.g. the book's *rashi bhavishya* is built from planets' gochar counted from the Moon, §14).
11. **Remedies** (§12.13): mantra, daan, gem, colour, worship.
12. **Compatibility** (§12.12) when relevant.

### 12.11 Reading the Samvat‑2081 chart as an example
The book's *varsha‑pravesh* kundali (p. 19) is the horoscope of the **start of the year (new moon, Delhi)**: **Mesh lagna** (book 29°35′; engine 29°12′), **Sun & Moon in Tula (7th house)**, Saturn in Kumbh (11th, own sign), Rahu in Meen (12th), Ketu in Kanya (6th), Jupiter in Vrushabh (2nd, retrograde), Mars in Kark (4th, debilitated), and – as read from the text – Venus (2nd lord) and Mercury in Vrushchik (8th).
The author reasons exactly as in §12.10: *lagna‑lord Mars debilitated in the 4th → public health and mood moderate, haste spoils work; 2nd‑lord Venus in the 8th aspecting the 2nd house → money matters stay controlled…* The same method yields the *Aavak–Javak* judgement (§14.4) and the monthly forecasts (p. 20).

### 15.2 Known limits of the reference engine
* **Sun accuracy ≈ 0.01°** → sankranti to ±15 min (±1 min for tithi). *Replace with VSOP87/Swiss for printing.*
* **No nutation** in the sidereal conversion (cancels with the mean ayanamsa) and **no light‑time/topocentric corrections** for the Moon (geocentric, as classical panchang).
* **ΔT** is a polynomial (error up to ~5 s in 2024, negligible).
* **Rahu is the mean node**; the book's day‑to‑day values may be the true node ⚠️.
* **Planets, eclipses, Asta, retro/direct stations** are not implemented in the pure‑Python file (see §4.9‑4.10).
* **Moonrise/Chandra‑darshan** not implemented (rule given in §6.5).
* The Hindu calendar has **school differences** (Surya‑siddhanta vs Drik, Vakya, Kerala/Tamil/Bengal, adhik‑kshaya selection, Smarta/Vaishnava). This README follows the **Drik (observational) Gujarati amānta** school used by the book.
* **Hijri / Parsi** dates are *rules*; actual religious dates depend on moon sighting.
